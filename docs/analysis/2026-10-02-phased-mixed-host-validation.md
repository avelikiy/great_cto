## Limits

These live runs prove bounded mixed-host dispatch, independent verification,
rework and human-gate stops on small fixtures. They do not measure product quality
uplift, establish full lifecycle delivery or establish release/install parity.

Beads. Independent security review, representative benchmark and actual delivered
artifact verification remain required before claiming complete support.
