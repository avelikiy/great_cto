06 — Open
What the spec cannot settle alone
Six findings, each measured rather than felt, each with the repair it implies. None is applied — a specification that quietly changed the product would be the thing this project exists to prevent.
FIND-1
a11y
--status-backlog is the only token under the AA floor.
#6b7280 on --bg-card measures 3.77 : 1 against a 4.5 floor for text. Every other status hue passes, most comfortably. As a 10px dot it is decoration and the floor does not apply; as the word "backlog" it does. Repair: lighten to ≈ #7d848f, or rule that this token is dot-only.
FIND-2
palette
Three greens mean "good"; two of them are the same colour.
--status-review and --status-done are both #00d97e — a card in review is indistinguishable from a card that shipped. --verdict-ok #4ade80 is a third, near-miss green beside them. Repair: give review its own hue, or merge the two columns and say so.
FIND-3
palette
A two-step text scale that does not step.
--text2 #8a9a92 at 6.17:1 and --text3 #8b958c at 5.87:1 differ by 5% of luminance — indistinguishable side by side, so the hierarchy they encode is not visible to the reader. Repair: separate them to roughly 6.2 and 4.6, or delete one.
FIND-4
system
Type is tokenised. Spacing and radius are not.
Twelve --fs-* tokens govern every size — and there is no --sp-* or --r-* at all. The page carries gap at 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16px, and radii at 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 16 and 999. Some of that is deliberate; some is one declaration at a time. Repair: a scale (4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24) and a ratchet on new raw values — the pattern already used for orphaned docs and hardcoded colours.
FIND-5
type
Synthesised bold on the mono face, in five rules.
Geist Mono is vendored with a wght 400–500 axis and asked for 600 in five places, so the browser draws a fake bold. Detail on artboard 05. Repair: 500, or widen the vendored axis.
FIND-6
scope
The light theme was measured — in the source, not here — and it had failed.
This finding first read "neither was measured here", which was wrong in the one direction a specification cannot afford: the measurement is sitting in the file this document transcribed. index.html:110–120 records four light-theme values that had been failing against --bg-strong — --accent at 1.51:1 against a 3:1 floor, plus --accent-text 4.17, --p1-fg 4.04 and --p2-fg 4.09 against 4.5 — each since recomputed rather than eyeballed. What is genuinely unmeasured is share.html: a separate artefact, its own palette, sixteen type sizes.
How this specification was produced — and the five things it got wrong
Every colour, size, height, radius and ratio was read out of packages/board/public/index.html and assets/fonts/fonts.css. Contrast figures are WCAG 2.1 relative luminance computed over those exact values, not estimated. Nothing here proposes a redesign; where the source and the intent disagree, the finding names both and stops.

A review of this document against that source found five statements that were never transcribed at all. They are corrected above and listed here rather than quietly overwritten — a specification whose whole authority is "these numbers were read, not chosen" owes its reader the list.

· the nav rail was given as 208px. The source says 240px, and the string 208 does not occur in it — read off the mock instead of the stylesheet, which is the exact failure this document exists to prevent.
· prose measure was given as 72ch. The source uses 52ch and 70ch.
· the surface steps were called "5% apart". They are +3.7, +2.0, +2.0, +2.2 points of lightness, and not even.
· "depth is stacking, never shadow" contradicted twenty box-shadow uses and three shadow tokens — and contradicted this document's own components page.
· controls were called "below the 44px floor by design", omitting that @media (pointer: coarse) restores 44px where the floor applies.
