01 — The decision
One system, two grounds
Consistency between the landing and the board is sameness of rules, not sameness of background. This page is set in the direction it describes: monospace throughout, zero radius, no shadow anywhere.
The measurement that forced it
The brand green cannot serve both grounds at text weight.
#00d97e measures 1.79:1 on a light ground. Not a near-miss — a failure by a factor of three. Darkening it until it passes produces #007a45 at 5.2:1, which is a different colour. That is physics, not preference, and it is what makes "one accent everywhere" impossible.

Six of eight rules are identical. That is a system. Linear runs a dark app with light modals on one vocabulary; the board already ships a light theme with 24 tokens, so half of this exists.
Why light on the landing, specifically
Measured against Refero: the top ten dark developer landings — Depot, Warp, Supabase, Trunk, Checkly, Cron, Linear, Eraser — all use a near-black canvas, most use surface shifts instead of shadows, and four use one green accent sparingly. Our landing is four for four. The ground is the single variable that removes the resemblance. The board keeps its dark because one operator reads it all day, and nobody chooses a tool for a distinctive admin.
